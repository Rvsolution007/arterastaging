# Retail Category - Products Addition Plan

Ye document un saari Sub-Categories aur Business Types ko list karta hai jisme abhi 0 products the, aur hum unme kya logical products add karenge. Jo Business Types apni Sub-Category se bilkul un-related lagte hain (jaise Mobile Accessories me Networking), unhe humne niche alag se (Unrelated section me) nikala hai.

---

## 2. Unrelated / Illogical Business Types (Separated)
Niche diye gaye Business Types "Mobile Accessories Store" ke andar add kiye gaye hain, jo ki illogical hain (Ye sab IT/Networking service providers ya B2B companies hain, na ki normal Retail shop accessories). 

**Sub-Category:** `Mobile Accessories Store` ke andar ye types galat jagah par hain:
- Workstation Dealer
- Business Computers
- Office Networking
- Campus Networking
- Data Networking
- Network Equipment Dealer
- Wi-Fi Solution Provider
- Enterprise Wi-Fi
- Hotel Wi-Fi
- Campus Wi-Fi
- Smart Wi-Fi

*Suggestion: Inhe "IT & Networking Solutions" ya "Computer Services" jaisi kisi alag service category me shift karna chahiye (abhi ke liye hum inme koi dummy product add nahi karenge).*
